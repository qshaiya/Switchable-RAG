ACME Corp Remote Work Policy (Sample)

1. Eligibility
Full-time employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are
eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week.

2. Core Hours
All remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 JST for meetings
and collaboration, regardless of their location.

3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monthly stipend of 5,000 JPY toward home
internet costs. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure network.

4. Data Security
Company documents must never be stored on personal cloud accounts. All work must
remain within approved systems. Confidential documents may not leave the
corporate network.

5. Requesting Remote Days
Remote days must be requested through the HR portal at least 24 hours in advance
and approved by the employee's direct manager.
